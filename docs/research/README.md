# Research evidence included with the framework

Start with [INTERPRETATION.md](INTERPRETATION.md). Detailed method reports and interpretation notes are preserved in each technique's folder. The spatial report remains the original three-clip pilot; the four other techniques include extended controls and analysis.

These are archived findings from the same selected KARD model, not claims that every new model or camera input will behave similarly. The portable methods in `src/kinetic_lens/xai/` execute new explanations; running them once does not reproduce the entire original research protocol.

The studies use 108 reused validation clips from two subjects. Their strongest findings concern sequence sensitivity, exact affine-head score accounting and conditional internal-channel effects. Concept semantics remain partially validated, spatial checks are mixed, and complete isolated action circuits were not established. The extra channel-retention selectivity experiment was result-triggered and is labeled exploratory.

Numeric vector figures are in `figures/`. Raw videos, subject frame images, feature/activation caches and large experiment bundles are not redistributed here. The full local research bundle remains available separately on the research machine. Local paths in archived Markdown were replaced by placeholders. Old experiment-specific commands are historical, not the portable framework entry point.

The [independent-semantic-validation protocol](SEMANTIC_VALIDATION_NEXT.md) is proposed future work, not an executed experiment. [parent_numerical_audit.json](parent_numerical_audit.json) records numerical consistency checks, not human concept validation.
